Why families order from Takwa

Takwa began in one home kitchen in Bangladesh with a simple rule: sell only what we would serve to our own family. That rule hasn't changed.

  • 🏡

    Made at home, by hand

    Every naru is rolled and every sandesh is pressed by hand, in small quantities, so it reaches you fresh.

  • 🌾

    Honest ingredients

    Coconut, gur, nuts, milk and spices. We tell you what goes into everything we make.

  • 🕌

    Halal, always

    Sourcing and preparation follow halal practice from start to finish. That's the meaning of takwa to us.

  • 📦

    Packed with care

    Sealed, cushioned and sent by courier to all 64 districts. Pay on delivery if you prefer.
